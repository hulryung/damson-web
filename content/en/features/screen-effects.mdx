# Same terminal. Different atmosphere.

From CRT glow to rain on glass: choose an effect and see the difference. Turn on **Show original alongside** for a side-by-side comparison.

<EffectGallery lang="en" />

## Try it in the app

Open Settings with `⌘,` and choose an effect under **Screen Effect**. Adjust its intensity or select **None** to return to the original view.

- **CRT and Trinitron**: scanlines, glow and RGB stripes evoke vintage monitors.
- **Green and amber phosphor**: glowing monochrome terminals.
- **VHS**: color fringing and static grain create a worn-tape texture.
- **Grayscale, sepia and blueprint**: monochrome, warm vintage and cool blue palettes.
- **Bloom, pixelate and invert**: soft light, chunky pixels and negative colors.
- **Rain, snow and underwater**: moving droplets and refraction, drifting flakes, waves and bubbles.

The three animated effects redraw continuously. Example videos do not autoplay when your browser requests reduced motion.

## Give your text some motion, too

Damson also offers fade, pop, slide, typewriter, flip, drop, dissolve, burn, glitch and colorful burst animations when glyphs appear or disappear. This gallery demonstrates **full-screen effects**; glyph animations are separate options in the app settings.
